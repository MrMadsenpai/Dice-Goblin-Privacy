# Privacy Policy for Dice Goblin
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Dice Goblin** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. The app is built with privacy by design: it collects **no personal information**, tracks no user behavior, and immediately purges transient networking data.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Specifically:
- **No Accounts:** No email addresses, user names, or profile credentials.
- **No Device Tracking:** No persistent hardware IDs, advertising IDs, IP logging, or geolocation data.
- **No Analytics or Ads:** No third-party behavioral trackers or ad network SDKs.

### 2. Local Save Data
All collected dice, timers, unlocked items, and pack-opening records are stored locally on your device. We have no external access to this data, and it is permanently erased if you clear the app data or uninstall the game.

### 3. Online Trading & Data Purging
When using the trade room feature:
- Only the minimal payload required to synchronize the swap (the temporary room code, temporary session identifier, and the list of offered dice) is transmitted to our cloud relay.
- **Zero Retention:** Data passing through the trade relay exists strictly for the duration of the active exchange. Trade records are immediately and permanently deleted upon trade completion, cancellation, or session expiration. No logs, trade histories, or persistent inventories are kept on any server.

### 4. Push Notifications
Notifications reminding you when a pack is ready to open are scheduled and executed locally by your device’s operating system. No device tokens or push notification data are transmitted to external servers.

### 5. Third-Party Infrastructure
The application uses minimal third-party cloud infrastructure strictly for relaying active trades:
- **Supabase:** Serves as the temporary database relay. No user records or personal identifiers are stored. You can review Supabase's privacy practices at https://supabase.com/privacy.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Dice Goblin does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It complies with all applicable privacy standards, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
